Privacy notice

Last updated: 8 November 2026 · Español

Who is responsible?

Cae Schlick operates the UNIO initiative in a personal capacity and is the controller of information submitted through this website. Contact hello@unio.today about your information or any privacy concern.

What we collect and why

The interest form collects your name, email address, participation category, optional message, contact consent, optional update preference and submission time. We also record a simple source category, such as the English or Spanish website or a tagged Instagram link, to understand where registrations come from. We do not store full referring URLs or use advertising cookies for this attribution.

We use these details to review and respond to your expression of interest and understand what people want UNIO to build. We send development updates only if you separately choose them. Please do not include sensitive personal information in your message.

Your choices and our basis for using information

For new website registrations, we rely on the consent you give to use your details to respond to your expression of interest. Consent to optional updates is separate and is not required. You can withdraw either consent at any time by emailing hello@unio.today. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out lawfully. Providing information is voluntary; without your name, email and contact consent, we cannot register and respond to your interest through the form.

Submitting the form does not create membership, ownership or a right to profits.

Who handles the information

Registrations are stored privately in Supabase. Vercel hosts the website, and the connected email service is used to handle correspondence. These providers may process connection and security information to operate their services. Registration records are not made public, sold or shared with other participants. Information may be disclosed if required by law.

Service providers may process technical information outside the UK. Contact hello@unio.today for information about the providers and applicable transfer arrangements. We do not claim that all technical processing takes place in the UK.

How long information is needed

We keep an expression of interest while it is needed to handle your enquiry or an ongoing conversation about participating in UNIO. The criteria are whether the enquiry is still open, whether you remain in contact and whether there is a continuing need for the record. Optional update details are needed while your subscription is active. When these purposes end, information should be deleted or anonymised, except for a minimal record needed to honour an opt-out or meet a legal obligation. You can request deletion at any time.

Your rights and complaints

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a portable copy of your information. You can withdraw consent and object to direct marketing. Email hello@unio.today to make a request or complaint. We do not make solely automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.
