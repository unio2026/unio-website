01 / The idea

You make a business work.
You should share in its success.

Customers bring the business. Workers make it happen. UNIO proposes a different relationship: useful products and services, fair pay for work, and a share of distributable profit for both groups.

The next step is listening. What do you need? What could you offer? Your answers can help identify a practical starting point.

Where we are today
UNIO is in development. Services, participation rules and the legal structure are still to be established.
02 / The proposed model

One business.
Shared success.

Profit sharing starts with a viable business. Here’s the proposed flow, from everyday transactions to participation in its success.

01 — PARTICIPATE

Useful things. Real needs.

Customers choose products and services. Workers deliver them and receive wages for their work.

02 — BUILD SUSTAINABLY

Cover what it takes.

Revenue pays operating costs, wages, taxes and agreed reserves or reinvestment.

03 — SHARE THE RESULT

Success comes back.

Any distributable profit would be shared between eligible customers and workers under published rules.

A proposed 50 / 50 split

Two groups. An equal stake in the pool.

The customer share would reflect qualifying spending. The worker share would reflect qualifying hours. Wages remain separate.

50%For eligible customers
50%For eligible workers
£20,000→ £10,000 in each pool

Illustration only, not a forecast. There are no current profit-sharing entitlements or guaranteed payments. Final eligibility, accounting and governance rules remain to be established.

A few things to know
Is UNIO operating today?

UNIO is an initiative in development. The website introduces the proposed model and invites expressions of interest. Participating services and profit-sharing arrangements have not yet been established.

Does 50/50 mean half of all sales?

No. The proposal concerns distributable profit after applicable costs, obligations and agreed reserves or reinvestment. It is not a split of sales revenue.

What does registering interest mean?

It starts a conversation. It does not establish membership, ownership, eligibility for distributions or any financial entitlement.
