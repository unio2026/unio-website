Ideas / A different way of doing business

What if workers shared in the success they help create?

Wages compensate work. Profit sharing asks whether workers should also participate in the surplus that remains after a business meets its obligations.

Pay and profit are different

A wage is compensation for work under an employment arrangement. Profit is what remains after relevant costs and obligations, and a distributable amount may be smaller still once reserves and reinvestment are considered.

Common ways businesses share success

Businesses may use bonuses, gainsharing, employee ownership or profit-sharing plans. Their rules vary, and not every arrangement gives workers ownership or guarantees payments.

UNIO's proposed hours-based model

UNIO is exploring a separate workforce pool equal to half of future distributable profit. Eligible workers would share that pool according to qualifying hours worked: one qualifying hour represents one unit. Wages would remain separate.

What is not yet decided

The legal arrangements, qualifying roles and hours, accounting rules and timing have not been established. UNIO does not currently offer a workforce profit-sharing entitlement.
